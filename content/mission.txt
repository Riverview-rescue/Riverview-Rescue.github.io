[What we believe]
Small label: What we believe
Quote: Every sentient being has feelings, emotions, family. All deserve a happy life.
Lead: That sentence is the whole of it. A cow is not a product that happens to breathe. She is somebody: curious, cautious, playful, stubborn, loyal. She knows her friends and she knows her calf.
Text: Our mission is simple. We take in cows who have nowhere else to go: the sick, the old, the neglected, the abandoned, and those bound for slaughter. We keep them fed, sheltered, cared for and known, for as long as they live. And by sharing their stories, we hope more eyes open to who these animals really are.

[Who they are]
Small label: Who they are
Headline: What the science says about cows.
Card: Friendship | They have best friends. | In a study of dairy cattle at the University of Northampton, cows penned with a preferred companion had lower heart rates and were calmer than when penned with a cow who wasn't a close companion.
Card: Family | Mothers and calves bond. | When a bonded cow and calf are separated early, researchers record both searching and calling for each other. At Riverview, they don't have to.
Card: Time | They are built to live decades. | A cow's natural lifespan is 15 to 20 years, and some live far longer. Big Mama reached 29, more than fifteen times the life of a typical beef steer.


[The scale of it]
Small label: The scale of it
Statement: 29.8 million cattle were slaughtered in the United States in 2025. Riverview has saved *thirty-two.*
Text: It is a small number beside a very large one. But to each of those thirty-two, it is everything. And we hope everyone who comes to know them sees cows a little differently afterwards.
